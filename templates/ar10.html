1. What is the Quotex Pakistan Time Zone?

Quotex uses the UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) system for chart timing and trade execution. Pakistan’s official local time zone is:

Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) = UTC +5

This means Pakistan is 5 hours ahead of UTC. Example:

  • UTC 10:00 = Pakistan 15:00 (3 PM)
  • UTC 00:00 = Pakistan 05:00 (5 AM)

2. Correct Quotex Time Zone for Pakistani Traders

The correct setting inside Quotex for Pakistan is:

→ GMT +5 (Asia/Karachi)

If your Quotex platform is showing wrong timing, it is usually because your system or browser is using:

  • Wrong time format
  • VPN time zone conflict
  • Mobile auto-time disabled
  • Browser caching old settings

7. How to Fix Quotex Wrong Time Zone Problem (100% Working)

Method 1: Fix on Windows PC

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Click Time & Language
  3. Enable Set time automatically
  4. Enable Set time zone automatically
  5. Select (UTC +05:00) Islamabad, Karachi

Method 2: Fix on Android Mobile

  1. Go to Settings → Additional Settings
  2. Tap Date & Time
  3. Enable Use Network-Provided Time
  4. Enable Use Network-Provided Time Zone

Method 3: Fix on iPhone (iOS)

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap General
  3. Select Date & Time
  4. Enable Set Automatically

Method 4: Clear Browser Cache (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome Settings
  2. Go to Privacy & Security
  3. Clear cache + cookies
  4. Restart the browser

Method 5: Disable VPN

VPN can change your time zone to UAE, USA, India, or Singapore. Disable VPN to avoid trading delays.

8. Quotex Time Zone Issues on Mobile App

If your Android or iPhone shows wrong candle timing:

  • Restart the app

Most cases are fixed immediately after correcting system time.
