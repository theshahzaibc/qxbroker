Quotex Live/Real Market Open Time in Pakistan — Complete Guide (2026)

Quick summary: Learn the exact live market hours on Quotex in Pakistan (PKT), the best sessions to trade, how OTC differs, and the ideal windows for Forex, indices, gold and crypto.

What is the Quotex “Live” or “Real” Market?

The Quotex live/real market links platform prices to global financial markets — forex, stocks, indices and commodities — and is active when those underlying exchanges are open. When global markets close, Quotex may still offer OTC (over-the-counter) instruments, but those behave differently than live market assets.

Quotex Live Market Session Times — Pakistan (PKT)

The following session times are converted to Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) to help traders in Pakistan schedule trades more accurately.

Asian Session (Tokyo / Sydney overlap)

  • Opens: 06:30 AM PKT
  • Closes: 02:00 PM PKT
  • Best for: JPY and AUD pairs; lower-to-medium volatility

European Session (London)

  • Opens: 12:00 PM PKT
  • Closes: 08:00 PM PKT
  • Best for: EUR, GBP pairs; higher liquidity and volatility

US Session (New York)

  • Opens: 05:00 PM PKT
  • Closes: 02:00 AM PKT
  • Best for: USD pairs, indices (S&P, NASDAQ), commodities (Gold)

London–New York Overlap — Prime Time

05:00 PM – 10:00 PM PKT — highest liquidity, tight spreads and reliable price moves. This is the best window for many short-term Quotex strategies (1-min, 5-min) and for indices/major pairs.

OTC, Crypto & Weekend Trading

OTC: Often available 24/7 on Quotex; useful for weekend practice but be aware price behavior differs from live markets.
Crypto: 24/7 trading — volume varies, and high overnight moves can occur. For crypto, monitor volatility rather than session time.

Best Times to Trade by Asset (PKT)

  • Forex majors (EUR/USD, GBP/USD): 05:00 PM – 10:00 PM PKT
  • Indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ): 05:00 PM – 01:00 AM PKT
  • Gold (XAU/USD): 02:00 PM – 10:00 PM PKT
  • Cryptocurrency: 24/7 — watch hourly volume spikes
  • OTC: 24/7 (weekends included)

Why trading at the right market time improves results

  1. Higher liquidity → tighter spreads and cleaner price action
  2. Clearer trend formation → better signal reliability
  3. Lower risk of false breakouts during thin markets
  4. Better execution and less slippage

Quick tips for Pakistani traders

  • Use PKT clock and convert session times when daylight saving changes in other regions.
  • Prefer London–NY overlap for short-term Quotex strategies.
  • Avoid low-volume Asian hours for major pair scalps unless you trade JPY/AUD pairs.
  • Check economic calendar for high-impact events (NFP, CPI, central bank decisions) that change volatility.

FAQ

Does Quotex work 24/7 in Pakistan?

Quotex operates 24/7 for OTC & crypto instruments, but live/real market assets follow global exchange hours (Mon–Fri).

What is the single best time to trade on Quotex in Pakistan?

The London–New York overlap: 05:00 PM – 10:00 PM PKT.

Are weekend trades on Quotex reliable?

OTC weekend trades are available but can be less predictable; use smaller position sizes and stricter risk controls.
