Is Quotex Withdrawal Working in Pakistan?

Yes — Quotex withdrawals work in Pakistan, but many traders report delays or rejections. Common causes include incomplete KYC, mismatched payment details, bank/wallet limits, bonus conditions, and security flags (like VPN use). Understanding and preventing these causes solves most problems.

1. Common Quotex Withdrawal Problems in Pakistan

Below are the top problems Pakistani traders experience when withdrawing from Quotex.

1.1 Withdrawal Not Showing / Stuck on Pending

Withdrawals can stay "Pending" for long periods because of:

  • Incomplete or failed KYC verification
  • High withdrawal volume or platform delays
  • Wrong or mismatched wallet/mobile number
  • Anti-fraud review on the account

1.2 Quotex Rejecting Withdrawal Requests

Typical reasons for rejection:

  • Name on ID not matching wallet name
  • Unfulfilled bonus turnover requirements
  • VPN use or suspicious login patterns
  • Multiple accounts or policy violations
  • Bank/wallet details not matching account records

1.3 EasyPaisa / JazzCash Withdrawal Not Working

Mobile wallets are common but introduce problems when the receiving number is wrong or not CNIC-linked. Issues include:

  • Wrong mobile number
  • Wallet not upgraded (Level 1 vs Level 2)
  • Daily receiving limits reached
  • Wallet suspended or inactive

Fix: verify the CNIC linkage, upgrade the wallet if needed, and always use your own wallet number.

1.4 Crypto Withdrawal Issues (USDT, BTC)

Crypto problems usually stem from network or address mistakes:

  • Wrong network selected (TRC20 vs ERC20)
  • Incorrect wallet address
  • Exchange deposit maintenance
  • Minimum withdrawal not met

1.5 Verification (KYC) Problems

Quotex requires clear, unedited documents. Rejections often come from blurry images, edited scans, or mismatched names.

1.6 Bonus Turnover Requirement

If you accepted a bonus, Quotex usually requires a turnover before allowing withdrawals. Example: a 50% deposit bonus often comes with high volume requirements that must be met.

2. Why Quotex Withdrawal Problems Happen in Pakistan (Real Reasons)

  • Strict banking and AML rules — banks and wallets scrutinize international inflows.
  • Anti-money laundering (AML) checks performed by the broker.
  • Incorrect personal details or mismatched documentation.
  • VPN or false location triggering security red flags.
  • Bonus rules requiring turnover.
  • Network choices for crypto (wrong network → loss of funds).

3. How to FIX Quotex Withdrawal Problems in Pakistan (Step-by-Step)

3.1 Complete Verification (KYC) Properly

Upload a clear CNIC front & back and a selfie holding the CNIC. Provide a recent bank statement or wallet screenshot if requested. Use good lighting, no filters or edits, and ensure names match exactly.

3.2 Use the Same Method for Deposit & Withdrawal

If you deposited via EasyPaisa, withdraw to EasyPaisa. Skrill deposits should be withdrawn back to Skrill, and crypto deposits back to crypto wallets.

3.3 Avoid Using VPN

Login from the same country you registered in (Pakistan). VPNs trigger fraud systems and lead to freezes or rejections.

3.4 Remove or Avoid Bonus If You Don’t Want Turnover

If you don’t want to meet a high turnover, cancel the bonus from the Active Bonuses section before trading.

3.5 Submit Correct Wallet Details

Use your CNIC-linked EasyPaisa/JazzCash number. Double and triple check digits before submitting.

3.6 Choose Correct Crypto Network

For USDT, prefer TRC20 (lower fees, faster), but ensure your receiving platform supports TRC20. Selecting the wrong network often causes permanent loss.

3.7 Try Smaller Withdrawals First

Start with $10–$20 to confirm the flow — successful small withdrawals help prevent bigger problems later.

6. Is Quotex Safe for Pakistani Traders?

Quotex is used internationally and is safe when you follow best practices: complete KYC, use verified wallets, avoid multiple accounts, and protect login credentials. The biggest issues are avoidable human errors and policy violations.

7. Tips to Avoid Withdrawal Problems in the Future

  • Keep account details updated and accurate.
  • Complete KYC immediately after signup.
  • Withdraw small amounts regularly to build trust.
  • Do not leave large sums in the trading account for long periods.
  • Never share your account or wallet with others.
  • Keep screenshots and transaction records for disputes.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Most delays happen due to verification issues, mismatched details, or high traffic.

No. It must match your CNIC and Quotex account details.

Check that you selected the correct network (TRC20 vs ERC20) and that the exchange supports that network.

How to fix "Withdrawal Rejected" issue?

Is Quotex withdrawal safe in Pakistan?

Yes, if you follow the correct steps: complete KYC, use verified wallets, and avoid sharing account access.
