Why Is Quotex Account Verification Necessary in Pakistan?

Verifying your Quotex account provides:

  • ✅ Safe & secure withdrawals to Pakistani wallets/banks

Documents Accepted for Quotex Verification in Pakistan

You can verify your Quotex account in Pakistan using any one of the following:

  • CNIC – Computerized National Identity Card
  • NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis)
  • Pakistani Passport
  • Residence Permit
  • Driving License (Pakistan)

No selfie video verification is required for Pakistani users.

You only need to upload your identity document front & back (if applicable).

Pre-Verification Checklist (Must Do First) for Quotex Account Verification in Pakistan

Before starting the Quotex verification process, complete the following steps to avoid rejection:

Verify Your Email Address

Go to your email inbox and confirm the email sent by Quotex.

Update Personal Details Correctly

Inside Quotex account settings (Account Tab), fill these exactly as written on your ID:

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Full Address

Tip: If your spelling or date of birth doesn’t match your CNIC or Passport, the verification will be rejected.

How to Verify Quotex Account in Pakistan – Step-by-Step

Follow this simple process to complete verification in 2 minutes:

Step 1: Go to Documents Verification Section

  • Login to your Quotex account
  • Open Account Tab → Documents Verification
  • Click Upload Documents

❗ If you see "Unknown URL" error… Don’t panic — this happens sometimes. Just click Try Again, and the page will reload.



Step 2: Select Document Type & Country

You will see the page: “Select type and issuing country of your identity document”

  • Choose your document (CNIC, NICOP, Passport, Residence Permit, Driving License)
  • Select Pakistan as the issuing country
  • Click Continue

Step 3: Upload Your Documents

  • Upload the front side (and back side if required)
  • Make sure the image is clear (no screenshots, no blur, glare, or cropped edges)
  • Click Continue



Step 4: Automatic Review (1 Minute Only!)

Quotex will automatically review your document within 60 seconds.

If valid:

✅ Your account status will show Verified

If rejected:

❌ Re-upload with a clearer photo or correct details

FAQs — Quotex Account Verification in Pakistan

1. Which document is best to verify my Quotex account in Pakistan?

The fastest and most commonly accepted documents are CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) or a Pakistani Passport. NICOP, driving license, and residence permits are also accepted.

2. Do I need a selfie video (video KYC) to verify my Quotex account in Pakistan?

No. For users in Pakistan, Quotex verification does not require a selfie video. Uploading a clear photo of an accepted identity document is sufficient.

3. Can I verify using a family member’s CNIC or passport?

No. You must verify using your own government-issued identity document. Submitting someone else’s ID will result in rejection and may violate Quotex terms.

4. What should I do if I see an "Unknown URL" error when uploading documents?

If you see the "Unknown URL" error, click "Try Again". The upload dialog should reload and show the option "Select type and issuing country of your identity document". From there select your document type, choose Pakistan as the issuing country, and proceed to upload.

5. How long does Quotex verification take in Pakistan?

Once you upload clear and correct documents, the automatic review usually completes within about 1 minute. In some cases final approval may take a few minutes if manual review is needed.

6. What personal details must match my identity document?

Your first name, last name, date of birth, and address in the Account tab must exactly match the information on your ID. Mismatched spelling or DOB is a common reason for rejection.

7. Which Pakistani documents are accepted for Quotex account verification?

Accepted documents include: CNIC, NICOP (for overseas Pakistanis), Pakistani Passport, Residence Permit, and Driving License. Upload front (and back if requested) in a clear photo.

8. Is verification safe and necessary for withdrawals?

Yes. Verification secures your account and is required to complete withdrawals and increase account limits. Quotex uses document encryption and secure procedures to protect your personal data.
