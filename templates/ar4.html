OTC 1‑Minute “Reversal Zone + Volume Pressure” Strategy — Quotex

A compact, printable guide implementing the professional 1-minute OTC setup described to you. Important: This is an actionable trading strategy but not a guarantee. Read the disclaimer below and practice on a demo account first.

Timeframe: 1-minute Instruments: Quotex OTC Indicators: EMA10, EMA50, RSI(14)

Quick Disclaimer

No trading strategy can promise 90–100% accuracy. OTC markets are broker-constructed — they often show algorithmic patterns and fake breakouts. This strategy is designed to filter noise and target high-probability reversals. Backtest and demo-trade it extensively. Use proper risk management.

Concept (Why it works)

  • OTC charts repeat fake breakout & wick rejection patterns.
  • We trade algorithmic rejections at well-defined support/resistance zones.
  • RSI confirms exhaustion, EMAs provide bias.

Indicators (Simple)

  • EMA 10 — fast trend guide
  • EMA 50 — market bias
  • RSI (14) — oversold/overbought (30/70)

Step‑by‑Step Rules

  1. Mark strong S/R zones with 3+ touches (wicks preferred).
  2. Wait for price to revisit a marked zone. Do NOT enter immediately.
  3. Confirm with a long wick rejection candle that closes back inside EMA10.
  4. RSI must be at/near 30 for CALL, near/above 70 for PUT.
  5. Ensure the rejection wick is larger than candle body and no huge momentum candle is present.
  6. Enter at the open of the next 1‑minute candle; expiry = 1 minute.

When NOT to trade

  • During strong trending moves without clear wicks.
  • When candle bodies are very large (high momentum).
  • News spikes / highly volatile sessions.
  • Consolidation with many tiny candles inside a box.

Example (CALL)

Price revisits marked support zone (3 touches). A 1‑min candle makes a long lower wick, closes back inside EMA10. RSI dipped to 28. Enter CALL at open of next candle, 1‑minute expiry.

Accuracy & Realistic Expectations

With disciplined entry filters and good money management, many traders can achieve roughly 70–85% win rate in favorable OTC conditions. Expect drawdowns; never assume 90% or above as guaranteed.

Quick Tips & Anti-Patterns

  • Tip: Mark S/R on multiple timeframes (1m, 5m) to avoid false zones.
  • Anti-Pattern: Do not trade immediate retests after huge candles that pierce zones with long bodies.

Cheat Sheet — One Page

1) Mark Zone (3+ touches)
2) Wait for price to hit zone again
3) Look for long wick rejection (Wick > Body)
4) RSI: <=30 for CALL, >=70 for PUT
5) EMA10 should contain the move (close back inside)
6) Enter at next candle open, expiry = 1 minute
7) Use fixed % stake, document trade

Final Recommendations

Start on demo, record 100+ trades, calculate win-rate and expectancy. Adjust stake sizes to keep drawdown tolerable. If you want, I can produce a downloadable PDF or a printable checklist in landscape A4 format — tell me which you prefer.
