Many traders in Pakistan face the issue of Quotex account verification pending for several days. This problem is common in 2025 due to increased platform usage, strict KYC rules, and mistakes in submitted documents.

In this guide, you’ll learn exactly why verification gets stuck, how to fix it, and how to get fast approval for withdrawals and trading.

What Is Quotex Account Verification?

Quotex verification is a mandatory KYC (Know Your Customer) process to confirm your identity before withdrawals and secure trading. All Pakistani users must submit identity and address documents to activate full account features.

Why Quotex Account Verification Stays Pending in Pakistan

1. High Verification Traffic

Pakistan has one of the fastest-growing user bases on Quotex, causing review delays.

2. Blurry or Low-Quality CNIC Photos

If your CNIC text is unreadable, your verification will stay pending.

3. Mismatched Personal Details

If your name, address, or date of birth don't match your ID, Quotex pauses verification.

4. Expired Documents

Old or expired CNICs and licenses are rejected automatically.

5. Slow Manual Review

During market peaks, Quotex’s manual review team takes longer to approve Pakistani accounts.

6. VPN or IP Change Issues

Using VPNs creates suspicion, leading to delayed verification.

Required Documents for Quotex Verification in Pakistan

  • ✔ Valid CNIC (front & back)
  • ✔ Pakistani passport (optional alternative)
  • ✔ Driving License
  • ✔ Proof of Address (utility bill, bank statement)
  • ✔ Selfie for identity confirmation

How to Fix Quotex Verification Pending Issue (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Upload High-Quality Documents

Use proper lighting and a 12MP+ camera for clear CNIC photos.

Step 2: Match Details Exactly

Your name, CNIC number, and DOB must match your Quotex profile.

Step 3: Avoid VPNs

Always use your Pakistani IP address. VPN usage triggers verification delays.

Step 4: Re-upload Documents if Rejected

You can resubmit corrected documents if the previous set was unclear.

Step 5: Contact Quotex Support

If verification is pending for more than 3–5 days, contact support through: support@quotex.io or live chat.

Fast Verification Tips for Pakistani Traders

  • ✔ Take photos in daylight with no glare
  • ✔ Hold your CNIC close to the camera
  • ✔ Ensure no shadows cover the ID
  • ✔ Submit a clean utility bill (3 months old max)
  • ✔ Don’t upload screenshots—only original photos

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why is my Quotex verification still pending?

Mostly due to low-quality documents, mismatched details, or high KYC traffic in Pakistan.

2. How long does Quotex verification take?

Usually 1–3 days, sometimes 7 days during busy periods.

3. What documents are accepted?

CNIC, passport, driving license, and any legal proof of address.

4. Can I trade without verification?

Yes, but you cannot withdraw funds until KYC is complete.
