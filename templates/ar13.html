What are Quotex Live Signals?

Quotex live signals are real-time trade ideas that tell you which asset to trade (for example EUR/USD or BTC/USD), the suggested direction (CALL/PUT), and an expiry time. Signals can be generated manually by analysts, by community groups (Telegram/Discord), or automatically via indicators and AI-based tools.

They are especially popular among beginners who want trade ideas without building a full strategy from scratch.

Can Signals Really Be 90% Accurate?

Short answer: Sometimes — but not reliably long-term.

Here's what to expect:

  • Temporary spikes: In strongly trending markets, a well-confirmed signal can win 80–95% of trades in a short sample (e.g., 20 trades) — this creates sensational claims.
  • Long-term reality: Over hundreds or thousands of trades, most legitimate systems average 65–85% depending on timeframe, strategy, and market conditions.
  • Transparency matters: Channels claiming consistent 90% accuracy should provide verifiable logs with timestamps and outcomes — if they don't, treat claims skeptically.

Important: No signal system is immune to major news events, sudden liquidity shifts, or platform execution delays. Always combine signals with your own confirmation rules.

Best Time to Use Signals (Pakistan Time — PKT)

Signal accuracy improves during high-liquidity sessions. For traders in Pakistan (PKT / UTC+5), these are the best windows:

  • London Session: 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM PKT — high liquidity on Forex pairs.
  • New York Session: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM PKT — best for volatility and reliable 1–5 minute signals.
  • London & New York Overlap: 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM PKT — ideal for short-term high-accuracy signals.

Avoid low-volume hours and major news releases where slippage and false signals spike.

High-Accuracy Signal Confirmation Strategy (Practical)

This confirmation method is designed to increase the probability of winning signals. It can push short-term accuracy closer to the 80–90% range during trends — but remember: not guaranteed.

Indicators & Settings

  • EMA 10 (fast) & EMA 25 (slow)
  • RSI (14) — use 50 as the momentum threshold
  • MACD (12,26,9) — confirm histogram direction

CALL (BUY) Rules

  1. EMA 10 is above EMA 25 (trend confirmation)
  2. RSI > 50 (momentum)
  3. MACD histogram is positive or rising
  4. Signal candle closes above recent resistance (use 1–2 minute expiry for Quotex)

PUT (SELL) Rules

  1. EMA 10 is below EMA 25
  2. RSI < 50
  3. MACD histogram is negative or falling
  4. Signal candle closes below recent support

Expiry & Trade Size

Use 1–2 minute expiries for 1-minute strategies. Risk 1–3% of your balance per trade and avoid Martingale — it amplifies losses.

Risk Management & Money Rules

  • Fixed risk per trade: 1–3% of account balance.
  • Daily loss cap: stop trading after 4–6 consecutive losses or 5% daily drawdown.
  • Demo testing: run the strategy for at least 100 demo trades before going live.
  • Position sizing: calculate stake based on volatility and account size.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Blindly following signals without confirmation.
  • Trading during major economic news or illiquid hours.
  • Using unverified Telegram channels with no track record.
  • Overleveraging or using aggressive Martingale to chase wins.
  • Ignoring slippage, spreads, and platform execution delays.

FAQs — Quick Answers

Q: Are free signals safe to use on Quotex?

A: Yes — if you verify the source, test on demo, and combine each signal with your own confirmation rules.

Q: How can a signal reach 90% accuracy?

A: Short-term market trends and strict confirmation filters can produce very high win rates temporarily. Long-term 90% consistency is not realistic.

Q: Where should I test signals?

A: Always start on a Quotex demo account. Backtest systematically and log every trade.

Q: Should I pay for premium signals promising 90%?

A: Be extremely cautious. Only pay if the provider offers verifiable trade history and a trial period. Otherwise, use free sources plus your own filters.

Conclusion

Quotex live free signals can be a valuable shortcut — especially for beginners — but claims of consistent 90% accuracy are usually exaggerated. Use reputable sources, confirm signals with trend and momentum indicators, trade during high-liquidity sessions (London/New York overlap), and apply strict money management. Start on demo, record your trades, and only scale up when you have a verified edge.
