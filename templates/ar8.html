How to Withdraw from Quotex in Pakistan — Step-by-Step Guide

A practical guide for Pakistani traders: withdrawal methods, processing times, common problems and fixes.

Overview — What is a Quotex Withdrawal?

A withdrawal on Quotex is the process of transferring your trading balance (profits) from your Quotex trading account to your chosen payout method (Easypaisa, JazzCash, local bank, or a cryptocurrency wallet). Withdrawals require account verification and correct payment details to avoid delays.

Minimum Withdrawal Amount

The minimum withdrawal amount on Quotex is $10 USD (or equivalent). Quotex itself typically does not charge withdrawal fees on standard methods, but third-party or network fees (like blockchain network fees for crypto) may apply.

Available Withdrawal Methods in Pakistan

  • Easypaisa — fast, widely used by Pakistani traders.
  • JazzCash — instant-to-few-hours payouts; mobile-first.
  • Local Bank Transfer — works for major Pakistani banks; longer processing time.
  • Cryptocurrency (USDT, BTC, etc.) — fastest global option if you can use a crypto wallet.

Step-by-step: How to Withdraw from Quotex in Pakistan

  1. Log in to your Quotex account with your email or social login.
  2. Go to the Withdrawal page: click your balance (top-right) then select Withdraw.
  3. Choose the withdrawal method (Easypaisa, JazzCash, Bank, Crypto).
  4. Enter amount (minimum $10) and fill receiver details:
    • Easypaisa / JazzCash: mobile number and account holder name.
    • Bank: bank name, IBAN, and full name.
    • Crypto: wallet address and network (e.g., TRC20 for USDT).
  5. Confirm the withdrawal request. You will receive a notification or email with the withdrawal status.
  6. Wait for processing — see processing times table below. If delayed, follow troubleshooting tips in the "Problems" section.

Common Quotex Withdrawal Problems in Pakistan & How to Fix Them

1. Account Not Verified

Issue: Withdrawals are held or rejected if your Quotex account is not fully verified.

Fix: Upload a clear CNIC/National ID, proof of payment method (screenshot of Easypaisa/JazzCash if required), and a selfie if requested. Wait for verification confirmation before requesting withdrawal.

2. Name Mismatch

Issue: The name on your Quotex profile must exactly match your CNIC and the payout account name (bank/Easypaisa/JazzCash).

Fix: Edit your payment profile or contact support to correct small spelling differences. Use the exact official name format from your CNIC/IBAN record.

3. Wrong Payment Details

Issue: Incorrect mobile number, IBAN, or crypto address leads to rejection or lost funds.

Fix: Double-check all digits and copy/paste addresses when available. For crypto, confirm network compatibility (TRC20 vs ERC20).

4. Using Different Payment Method Than Deposit

Issue: Some withdrawals are blocked if the withdrawal method differs from the deposit method (to prevent fraud).

Fix: Withdraw using the same method you used to deposit. If you must change, contact Quotex support with proof and follow their instructions.

5. Large Withdrawal Requiring Manual Approval

Issue: High-value withdrawals may be held for manual review.

Fix: Provide requested KYC documents and split large withdrawals into smaller amounts if permitted.

6. Payment Provider Downtime

Issue: Easypaisa or JazzCash maintenance can cause delays.

Fix: Try again after a few hours or choose crypto/bank transfer as an alternative.

Pro tip: Keep screenshots and transaction IDs for any failed or delayed withdrawals — they help support resolve issues faster.

Is Withdrawing from Quotex Safe in Pakistan?

Yes — withdrawals are safe when you follow Quotex's rules: complete KYC, use correct payment details, and withdraw to verified accounts. Use crypto if you need the fastest option, but always verify addresses before sending/receiving.

FAQs

1. What is the minimum withdrawal on Quotex in Pakistan?
The minimum withdrawal amount is $10 USD (or equivalent).
2. How long does a Quotex withdrawal take in Pakistan?
Easypaisa/JazzCash: typically 12–24 hours. Bank transfers: 1–3 business days. Crypto: usually 5–30 minutes depending on the network.
3. Does Quotex charge a withdrawal fee?
Quotex generally does not charge withdrawal fees on common methods, but banks, local services, or crypto networks may apply fees.
4. Why is my withdrawal pending or rejected?
Common reasons: unverified account, name mismatch, wrong payment details, or different withdrawal method than deposit. Check messages from Quotex support for exact reason.
5. Can I withdraw to a different Easypaisa/JazzCash number than the one I used for deposit?
Usually Quotex requires deposits and withdrawals to use the same payment method. If you need to change, contact support with clear documentation.
6. What is the fastest method to withdraw from Quotex?
Cryptocurrency (e.g., USDT on TRC20) is often fastest, followed by Easypaisa and JazzCash for Pakistani users.
7. What should I do if a withdrawal doesn't arrive?
Check your Quotex account status and email notifications first. If still unresolved, contact Quotex support with the withdrawal ID and screenshots of the request.
